Origin & Name
🕰 Origin Story

How Pax Silica
Came to Be

Born out of growing geopolitical anxiety over semiconductor supply chains, Pax Silica was conceived as the West's answer to technological overdependence — particularly on China — for chips, rare earths, and AI infrastructure.

December 2025
Washington D.C. Summit — The non-binding Pax Silica Declaration is signed by 9 founding nations after a high-level summit convened by the United States.
February 20, 2026
AI Impact Summit, New Delhi — India formally joins, becoming the 10th signatory and signaling a major geopolitical pivot.
Ongoing · 2026
Expansion & Implementation — Working groups establish full-stack tech coordination across minerals, fabrication, AI, and talent pipelines.
🔤 Decoding the Name
PAX
Peace
From Latin — evokes stability, cooperation, and an end to coercive technological dependency
SILICA
Silicon
Silicon dioxide — the raw substrate of every semiconductor, the literal foundation of the digital age
Together, Pax Silica expresses a vision of peace through silicon — the idea that democratic nations can build a stable, prosperous, and secure global technology order free from single-point coercion or dependency.
03
Core Goals
🎯 Strategic Objectives

What Pax Silica
Sets Out to Achieve

Pax Silica is designed to build a "secure, resilient, and innovation-driven" technology ecosystem by coordinating across the full tech stack — from raw minerals to AI infrastructure — among trusted democratic partners.

🔗
Resilient Supply Chains
Diversify and secure global semiconductor supply chains by reducing reliance on any single nation's manufacturing dominance, particularly China.
🌐
Friend-Shoring
Redirect critical tech production to trusted partner nations — creating mutually reinforcing economic ties among like-minded democracies.
⚛️
Full-Stack Coordination
Align partners across every tier: mineral extraction, refining, chip design, wafer fabrication, advanced packaging, and AI data centers.
🛡️
Reduce Coercive Dependency
Ensure no member nation can be economically coerced via control of rare earths, advanced nodes, or critical technology inputs.
🤖
AI Infrastructure Access
Jointly develop and deploy advanced AI infrastructure — compute clusters, cloud frameworks, and foundation models — within trusted borders.
🎓
Talent & Innovation
Build shared pipelines for STEM talent, research collaboration, and cross-border IP frameworks to sustain long-term innovation leadership.

05
India's Inclusion
🇮🇳
📅 February 20, 2026 · AI Impact Summit, New Delhi

India Joins
Pax Silica

While not among the original 9, India's formal accession in February 2026 marked a watershed moment — both for Pax Silica's geopolitical weight and for India's own ambitions in semiconductors, AI, and critical minerals.

🤝 Bilateral Addendum
Alongside signing the Declaration, India and the US inked a Joint Statement on the "India-U.S. AI Opportunity Partnership" — a bilateral addendum cementing AI co-development, compute access, and talent exchange between the two nations.
🚀 What India Gains
🏭
Semiconductor Mission Boost
Access to allied supply chains and technology transfers turbocharges India's domestic fab ambitions — accelerating the India Semiconductor Mission's goals for homegrown chip manufacturing.
⛏️
Critical Minerals Access
Membership opens India to coordinated rare earth and critical mineral supply chains from partners like Australia — reducing import vulnerability for EV and electronics sectors.
🤖
AI Infrastructure & Compute
India gains pathways to advanced GPU compute, AI model development frameworks, and joint research with leading AI nations — essential for its Digital India and AI mission.
👨‍💻
Talent Pipeline & FDI
India's vast STEM workforce is formally positioned as a strategic asset — attracting FDI in chip design centers, R&D labs, and advanced manufacturing from allied multinationals.
🌐
Geopolitical Elevation
Joining Pax Silica signals India's definitive alignment with the democratic tech bloc — reinforcing its role as a swing state that chose partnership over neutrality in the new tech cold war.
